# Licensing the Small Modular Reactor

**A course and an issue register.** Version 0.1, working draft.

Eclectic Technologies · T. Roxey · CC BY 4.0

---

There are two ways to learn how a small modular reactor gets licensed in the United States. You can read the framework — the parts of Title 10 that apply, the pathways an applicant can choose, the machinery the staff uses to review what arrives. Or you can read the arguments that are still unsettled, because those are where the framework is actually being built right now.

This site does both, and it insists on doing them together. The course half walks the licensing framework from orientation to a finished submittal. The register half holds the open issues, one entry apiece, each traced to the provision it lives under and the vehicle that would close it. Every module ends on the register entries it governs, so the method never stays abstract and the issues never float free of the law they belong to.

## Who this is for

Two readers at once, deliberately. Every page opens with a plain-language account of what is going on and why it matters, and then goes to the depth a practitioner needs. If you already know what an RAI is, skim the opening and keep reading. If you do not, the opening is there so the rest is legible.

That layering is not a courtesy. Reactor licensing is a public process conducted on a public docket, and its legitimacy depends on more than the applicant and the staff being able to follow it. An investor deciding whether a first-of-a-kind build is financeable, a policy staffer drafting around the ADVANCE Act, a reporter covering a siting fight, a neighbor at a town hall — each of them can reach an informed judgment about this material if the material lets them. So it lets them.

## What this is not

It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for the regulations. Where a provision governs, the provision governs, and the [Regulatory Index](resources/regulatory-index.md) points at the real text. Nothing here has been through NRC review.

It is also not a conformance argument dressed as a tutorial. The house rule of this site, stated fully in [Name the Gap, Name the Vehicle](bridge/module-6-assembling-a-submittal/03-name-the-gap-name-the-vehicle.md), is that where the record has a gap we say so and name what would close it. A site that made every issue look resolved would be useless to the only readers who matter.
