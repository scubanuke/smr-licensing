# Open Issues

Pathway selection looks like the most settled thing in licensing and currently is not, because one of the two modern frameworks it depends on is still a proposed rule.

**[LI-16 — The Part 53 / Part 57 band](../../register/LI-16-the-part-53-part-57-band.md)** — the near-term integral class sits between two frameworks and the boundary between them has not been stated in applicable terms. Until Part 57 concludes, an applicant near the line owns the placement argument.
