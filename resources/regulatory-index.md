# Regulatory Index

What this site cites, where it lives, and what it governs. Federal regulations, regulatory guides, NUREGs and Environmental Protection Agency material are United States government works and may be reproduced; **industry consensus documents are copyrighted and are characterized rather than excerpted** throughout this site, as [Conventions](../CONVENTIONS.md) requires.

Where a provision governs, the provision governs. Nothing on this site substitutes for reading it.

## Licensing pathways

**10 CFR Part 50** — domestic licensing of production and utilization facilities; the original two-step construction permit and operating licence.

**10 CFR Part 50, Appendix A** — the General Design Criteria. Those doing the most work here are the criteria on natural phenomena and dynamic effects, on sharing of structures, systems and components among units, on electric power systems, on protection-system reliability and testability, and on residual heat removal.

**10 CFR Part 52** — licences, certifications and approvals: design certification, early site permits, combined licences.

**10 CFR Part 53** — risk-informed, technology-inclusive framework for commercial nuclear plants. Final rule, March 2026, with a correction in April 2026.

**10 CFR Part 57** — proposed rule, May 2026: licensing requirements for microreactors and other reactors with comparable risk profiles. See [LI-16](../register/LI-16-the-part-53-part-57-band.md).

**10 CFR Part 100** — reactor site criteria. See [LI-13](../register/LI-13-geopolitical-siting.md) for what it does not reach.

## Emergency preparedness

**10 CFR 50.47 and Part 50 Appendix E** — emergency plans and planning standards; 50.47(c)(2) carries the legacy ten-mile plume and fifty-mile ingestion zones and the narrow case-by-case opening.

**10 CFR 50.160** — emergency preparedness for small modular reactors, non-light-water reactors, and non-power production or utilization facilities. Added by the 2023 rule; refers the zone determination to 50.33(g)(2).

**10 CFR 50.33(g)(2)** — the sizing criterion itself: whether projected public dose exceeds 1 rem total effective dose equivalent over 96 hours. See [M03-CN-01](course-notes/M03-CN-01-epz-sizing-criterion.md).

**Emergency Preparedness for Small Modular Reactors and Other New Technologies** — final rule, Federal Register, 16 November 2023. The performance-based, consequence-scaled planning zone rests here.

**EPA Protective Action Guides Manual (2017)** — the early-phase protective action guide the regulatory criterion aligns with. The binding criterion for zone sizing is in 50.33(g)(2), not in the manual.

## Source term

**Regulatory Guide 1.183** — alternative radiological source terms for evaluating design-basis accidents.

## Security

**10 CFR 73.1** — the design-basis threat.

**10 CFR 73.21 and 73.22** — protection of Safeguards Information.

**10 CFR 73.54** — protection of digital computer and communication systems and networks.

**10 CFR 73.55** — requirements for physical protection against radiological sabotage.

**Regulatory Guide 5.71** — cyber security programs for nuclear facilities.

**Industry cyber-security guidance** — the endorsed cyber-security plan template and the critical-digital-asset scoping guidance. *Copyrighted; characterized only on this site. Copies are obtainable through industry channels where a Course Note needs to characterize a specific section accurately.*

## Instrumentation and control

**IEEE 603** — criteria for safety systems of nuclear power generating stations. *Copyrighted; characterized only.*

## Statute

**ADVANCE Act** — the statutory driver behind the current wave of licensing modernization.

**Section 1602 counter-uncrewed-systems authority** (FAA Reauthorization Act of 2018) — the federal detect-and-defeat authority not extended to commercial reactor licensees. See [LI-01](../register/LI-01-counter-uas-defeat-authority.md).

**49 U.S.C. 40103** — sovereignty and use of airspace; the Federal Aviation Administration's deconfliction role in any counter-uncrewed-systems scheme.

**FAST Act §61003** — the precedent for a federal information-sharing and directive authority in the energy sector, cited as the structural analogue for the warning pathway in [LI-11](../register/LI-11-the-warning-pathway-and-who-decides.md).

## Grid

**Reliability standards on under-frequency load shedding and generator ride-through** — the grid regulator's instruments, on the far side of the jurisdictional seam described in [LI-14](../register/LI-14-under-frequency-load-shedding.md).

## International

**IAEA SF-1, SSR-2/1 and Nuclear Security Series 13** — carried in the corpus's international-track instruments rather than in the United States licensing case, and listed here so the citation resolves.
