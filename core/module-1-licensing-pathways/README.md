# Module 1 — The Licensing Pathways

## Learning Objectives

After this module, a reader will be able to:

- Distinguish the Part 50 two-step route from the Part 52 combined-licence route and say what each commits an applicant to.
- Place a given reactor design in the band served by Part 53 or by the proposed Part 57, and defend the placement.
- Explain why an applicant writes to topical-report standard, in financial rather than merely procedural terms.
- Identify which parts of the current framework are settled law and which are still moving.

## What this module does

An applicant does not simply apply. They choose a pathway, and the choice determines what has to be demonstrated, in what order, and how much of the demonstration can be reused by the next applicant. For a first-of-a-kind design, that last question is often the one the capital decision turns on.

This module lays out the four routes and then explains the mechanism — the topical report — that converts a regulatory cost into a referable asset.

## Units

**[Part 50 and Part 52](00-part-50-and-part-52.md)** — the original two-step construction permit and operating licence, and the certification-and-combined-licence structure built to remove the post-construction licensing risk that killed projects under the old route. Design certification, early site permits, and what a combined licence actually combines.

**[Part 53 and Part 57](01-part-53-and-part-57.md)** — the technology-inclusive framework, and the proposed rule for microreactors and comparable risk profiles. Where the near-term integral small modular reactor class sits relative to both, and why it sits above the low-consequence band Part 57 is drawn around.

**[Topical Reports](02-topical-reports.md)** — reviewed once, found acceptable, referable thereafter. The mechanism that explains most of the form the design-basis corpus takes, and the reason a wrapper with an applicability statement and a conformance matrix is not administrative overhead but the substance of the filing.

**[Snapshot — 2026 Rule Status](90-snapshot-2026-rule-status.md)** — the dated unit. Part 53 final, Part 57 proposed, the ADVANCE Act implementation, and the industry reform pressure behind all of it. This unit is expected to age fastest on the site and is refreshed on its own cycle; the durable pathway material above does not depend on it.
