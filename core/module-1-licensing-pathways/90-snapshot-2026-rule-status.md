# Snapshot — 2026 Rule Status

**Current as of 14 September 2026.** This unit is deliberately volatile and is refreshed on its own cycle. Nothing elsewhere on the site depends on it: the durable pathway material is in the units above, and this page carries only what will age.

If you are reading it long after the date above, treat it as a record of where things stood rather than as current fact, and check the primary sources named at the foot.

## The framework rules

**10 CFR Part 53** — the risk-informed, technology-inclusive framework — was published as a **final rule on 30 March 2026**, with a **correction on 13 April 2026**. Practice under it is still forming; there is not yet a deep body of precedent for how the staff applies it in a contested case.

**10 CFR Part 57** — licensing requirements for microreactors and other reactors with comparable risk profiles — was published as a **proposed rule on 1 May 2026**. It has not concluded. Until it does, the boundary between the two bands is not stated in terms an applicant can apply mechanically, which is [LI-16](../../register/LI-16-the-part-53-part-57-band.md).

The near-term integral light-water class, roughly seventy-seven to three hundred megawatts electric, sits above the low-consequence band Part 57 is drawn around.

## The conforming amendments are worth watching

When a framework rule is finalized, other parts of Title 10 are amended to reach the new licensees, and those amendments say something about what the Commission considers durable.

One example found in the course of writing this site: **10 CFR 73.58**, the safety/security interface requirement, was **amended on 30 March 2026** — the same day Part 53 was finalized — so that paragraph (a) now reads "part 50, part 52, or part 53." The obligation to assess and manage the effect of security changes on safety, and safety changes on security, was carried across to the new framework rather than left behind with the old ones.

That is a small detail with a clear signal in it: the interface concern was treated as fundamental rather than as an artifact of how the older parts happened to be organized. It is recorded in [Course Note M04-CN-01](../../resources/course-notes/M04-CN-01-safety-security-interface.md).

Anyone tracking this area should read conforming amendments rather than skipping to the headline rule. They are where you find out what survived.

## The statute driving the schedule

The **ADVANCE Act** directed much of this modernization, with deadlines attached. The pace of rulemaking and guidance over the last several years is substantially the pace of statutory implementation rather than of technical consensus forming.

Practical consequence: a rule or guidance document arriving on an unusual timetable is often answering a deadline. That is worth knowing before inferring that a settled view exists behind it.

## Industry-side activity

The consensus process continues to generate guidance that the Commission may endorse — the mechanism described in [Module 4](../../core/module-4-security-licensing/01-cyber-security-under-73-54.md), and the route by which the campus cyber questions at [LI-02](../../register/LI-02-campus-scaled-cda-identification.md) and [LI-03](../../register/LI-03-shared-digital-ic-guidance.md) would close.

The site's treatment of specific industry documents is thinner than it should be, for a reason stated openly in the [Course Notes convention](../../resources/course-notes/README.md): that guidance is copyrighted and is characterized rather than excerpted here, and characterizing accurately requires working from an actual copy rather than from secondary description. That is a known limitation of this site rather than a judgment about the guidance.

## What to check when refreshing this unit

Whether Part 57 has concluded and, if so, what band it states — which closes or reshapes [LI-16](../../register/LI-16-the-part-53-part-57-band.md).

Whether any further corrections or conforming amendments have issued against Part 53.

Whether emergency-preparedness or cyber-security guidance has been updated or newly endorsed — the campus items at [LI-02](../../register/LI-02-campus-scaled-cda-identification.md) and [LI-03](../../register/LI-03-shared-digital-ic-guidance.md) would show up here first.

Whether anything has moved on the counter-uncrewed-systems authority at [LI-01](../../register/LI-01-counter-uas-defeat-authority.md), which needs Congress rather than the Commission and will therefore not appear in a rulemaking.

Primary sources are listed in the [Regulatory Index](../../resources/regulatory-index.md); the Federal Register and the eCFR are where the answers are, and neither is behind anything.
